# S3XWÀRE

**S3XWÀRE** is a powerful and customizable Blade Ball script hub for Roblox, designed with competitive automation in mind. It includes advanced features for auto-parrying, AI-assisted gameplay, farming, and more.

## Features

- **Auto Parry** – Fast and accurate parrying based on ball and player data.
- **AI Play** – Intelligent bot behavior for dodging and chasing targets.
- **Manual & Auto Spam** – Two spam modes for low and high ping conditions.
- **Ball Visualizer** – Optional ball visualizer for better awareness.
- **Auto Farm** – Multiple orbit and pattern modes to grind automatically.
- **Custom Sounds** – Play custom hit sounds on parry.
- **Performance Tools** – Lag reducer, lighting optimizations, and texture removal.
- **Player Tweaks** – Flight, bunny hop, walk speed, gravity, FOV, and more.
